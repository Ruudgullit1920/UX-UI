# Authenticated acceptance audit summary

## Current result: 71.6 / 100 (7.2 / 10)

This is a bounded UX/UI assessment of one authenticated consultant workspace route plus the accepted public login and registration evidence. Six inspected pages and states provide visual coverage; they do not represent six unique product routes.

## Confirmed findings (6)

1. Workspace action controls extend outside the phone viewport.
2. Elements must meet minimum color contrast ratio thresholds.
3. Error states do not explain the failure or provide a recovery path.
4. Mixed French and English interface labels reduce language consistency.
5. Password visibility controls are unnamed and too small for reliable access.
6. Required registration fields are not communicated before input.
